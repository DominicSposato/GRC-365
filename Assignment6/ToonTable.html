Toon Table

The term “toon” is a shortened form of the word “cartoon.” It became popular as a nickname for animated cartoons and the characters who appeared in them, particularly during the growth of American animation in the 20th century. The word “cartoon” itself comes from the Italian cartone, meaning “large sheet of paper,” and originally referred to preliminary drawings or sketches made by artists. As animated films became more popular, “cartoon” came to mean short animated films, and eventually “toon” developed as an informal abbreviation. Today, “toon” is commonly used to describe animated characters, especially those with exaggerated or humorous designs.
